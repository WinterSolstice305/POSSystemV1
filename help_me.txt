MINI SHOPPING CART HELP

Main menu:
- [B] Browse products
- [V] View, edit, remove, or check out cart items
- [P] Update stock or manage products
- [H] Show this help screen
- [S] Show the current sales report
- [Q] Quit the system

Browsing products:
- The catalog displays each product's code, name, price, and available stock.
- Choose [A] to add a product to the cart, then enter its code and quantity.
- Choose [Q] to return to the main menu.
- The requested quantity must be positive and cannot exceed available stock.

Shopping cart:
- [C] Check out the cart.
- [E] Edit an item's quantity. Set it to 0 to remove the item.
- [R] Remove an item.
- [Q] Return to the main menu.

Checkout:
- A subtotal of at least ₱100 receives a 10% discount.
- You can also request the 20% senior/PWD/student discount.
- Discounts are applied in order, one after another.
- Enter payment when prompted. Insufficient payment is rejected.
- Product stock is reduced and the cart is cleared after successful checkout.

Product and stock management:
- From the main menu, choose [P].
- [E] Replace the stock quantity for a product.
- [A] Add a product with a unique code, name, price, and stock.
- [R] Remove a product after confirming.
- [Q] Return to the main menu.
- Stock and price must be non-negative values. Product names and codes cannot be blank.
